HR LEAVE POLICY SUMMARY
==================================================
[1.1] Governs permanent and contractual employees of the CMC.
[1.2] Excludes daily wage workers and consultants.
[2.1] 18 days paid annual leave per calendar year.
[2.2] Annual leave accrues at 1.5 days per month.
[2.3] Requires at least 14 calendar days advance notice using Form HR-L1.
[2.4] Must have written approval from direct manager before leave commences; verbal approval is not valid.
[2.5] Unapproved absence recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP) regardless of subsequent approval.
[2.6] Maximum of 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward; above 5 are forfeited on 31 Dec.
[2.7] Carry-forward days must be used in Q1 (Jan-Mar) or forfeited.
[3.1] 12 days paid sick leave per calendar year.
[3.2] 3+ consecutive sick days requires medical certificate within 48 hours of return.
[3.3] Sick leave cannot be carried forward.
[3.4] Sick leave before/after public holiday/annual leave requires medical certificate regardless of duration.
[4.1] 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for first two live births.
[4.2] 12 weeks paid maternity leave for third or subsequent child.
[4.3] 5 days paid paternity leave within 30 days of birth.
[4.4] Paternity leave cannot be split.
[5.1] LWP allowed only after exhausting applicable paid leave.
[5.2] LWP requires approval from both the Department Head AND the HR Director.
[5.3] LWP > 30 continuous days requires Municipal Commissioner approval.
[5.4] LWP does not count toward service for seniority, increments, or retirement benefits.
[6.1] Entitled to all gazetted public holidays declared by the State Government.
[6.2] Working on public holiday grants 1 compensatory off day (must use within 60 days).
[6.3] Compensatory off cannot be encashed.
[7.1] Annual leave encashment at retirement/resignation only (max 60 days).
[7.2] Leave encashment during service is not permitted under any circumstances.
[7.3] Sick leave and LWP cannot be encashed under any circumstances.
[8.1] Grievances must be raised with HR within 10 working days.
[8.2] Grievances after 10 working days not considered unless written exceptional circumstances demonstrated.
